EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
The Polish rail freight network is operated by PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe
(PKP PLK), the state-owned infrastructure manager. PKP PLK oversees
approximately 28 marshalling yards across the country, of which roughly a
dozen are classified as active hump yards. EU TEN-T co-funding (2021–2027)
unlocks a significant modernization window through 2027.

Active hump yards identified by OIU as priority targets for DECEL:

  1. Idzikowice Classification Yard (the priority target — declared
     end-of-life for the existing Axtone retarder; Q4 2026 modernization
     tender planned).

  2. Karsznice Sorting Yard (Knorr-Bremse hydraulic retarder installed
     2014; no modernization planned before 2028).

  3. Warszawa Praga Classification Yard (mixed Axtone + Knorr-Bremse;
     track group 3 modernized 2017; remaining track groups end-of-life
     by 2029).

  4. Gliwice Sorting Yard (Axtone retarder installed 2010; no
     modernization timeline yet).

  5. Łódź Olechów Sorting Yard (Voestalpine retarder installed 2012;
     no modernization timeline yet).

  6. Wrocław Brochów (additional yard identified in the mapping; modern
     retarder installed 2018; not yet a priority target).

PROCUREMENT CONTEXT
-------------------
The Idzikowice modernization tender is expected to be published in Q4 2026
with award in Q2 2027. Total contract value estimated at €40–60M including
retarders, control systems, and 24-month integration.
